# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What that means

- Criticise code, not people. "This leaks a handle" is useful; "this is garbage"
  is not.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are missing context, not bad intent.
- Accept that maintainers may say no. A small project stays maintainable by
  declining things, and that is not a personal judgement.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Rathio12/LuwanTerm/security/advisories/new), or by
direct message to [@Rathio12](https://github.com/Rathio12). Reports stay
private. Anything from a word in a thread to a ban is on the table, in
proportion to what happened.
